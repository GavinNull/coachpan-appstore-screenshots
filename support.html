常见问题

收不到验证码怎么办？

请确认手机号输入正确、手机信号正常，并留意短信是否被拦截。同一号码 60 秒内只能获取一次验证码，验证码 5 分钟内有效。仍然收不到请来信。

如何管理或取消订阅？

订阅通过 Apple 账号扣费，请在 iPhone「设置 - Apple 账户 - 订阅」中管理或取消。取消后当前周期内仍可正常使用，到期后不再续费。

换了手机，如何恢复已购买的订阅？

用同一个 Apple 账号登录，在 App 的订阅页面点击「恢复购买」即可。

如何注销账号、删除我的数据？

在 App 内「我的 - 设置 - 账号 - 注销账号」直接发起注销。注销会永久删除账号及全部训练记录、对话历史和健康数据，无法恢复。

训练建议可以替代医生或专业教练吗？

不能。小潘教练提供的训练建议仅供参考，不能替代专业医疗诊断或建议；如有身体不适请及时就医。

FAQ

I didn't receive the verification code.

Check that the phone number is correct and that your phone has signal, and check whether the SMS was filtered. A new code can be requested once every 60 seconds, and each code is valid for 5 minutes. If it still doesn't arrive, email us.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account. Go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone to manage or cancel. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the current period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap "Restore Purchases" (恢复购买) on the subscription screen in the app.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Me > Settings > Account > Delete Account (我的 - 设置 - 账号 - 注销账号). This permanently deletes your account, training records, chat history and health data.

Is the training advice a substitute for a doctor or coach?

No. Training suggestions are for reference only and are not medical advice. If you feel unwell, please seek medical attention.
